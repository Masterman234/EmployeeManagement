UNIVERSITY OF ILESA (UNILESA) - MEDICAL & HEALTH SCIENCES ADMISSION GUIDE

This document contains admission requirements, JAMB combinations, and estimated fresher school fees for all Medical, Clinical, and Allied Health Science programs available at the University of Ilesa.

----------------------------------------------------
1. CORE MEDICAL & HEALTH SCIENCE PROGRAMMES OFFERED
----------------------------------------------------
* B.N.Sc. Nursing Science
* B.MLS. Medical Laboratory Science
* B.Sc. Public Health
* B.Sc. Anatomy
* B.Sc. Physiology

*Note: UNILESA does not currently offer Medicine and Surgery (MBBS) or Doctor of Pharmacy (PharmD). The clinical programs offered are listed above.*

----------------------------------------------------
2. GENERAL ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS
----------------------------------------------------
* O'Level: Five (5) credit passes in WAEC, NECO, or NABTEB at not more than two sittings.
* Compulsory O'Level Subjects: English Language, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and Biology.
* JAMB Subject Combination: Use of English, Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (compulsory for all health courses).
* Age Requirement: Must be at least 16 years old by September 30 of the admission year.
* JAMB Choice: UNILESA must be chosen as your First Choice institution.

----------------------------------------------------
3. UTME CUT-OFF MARKS & COMPETITIVENESS
----------------------------------------------------
While the basic university baseline UTME cut-off is 160, medical courses are highly competitive and tier-based:
* Nursing Science: Highly competitive (200 - 240+ recommended)
* Medical Laboratory Science: Highly competitive (190 - 220+ recommended)
* Public Health / Anatomy / Physiology: Competitive (170 - 190+ recommended)

----------------------------------------------------
4. ESTIMATED FRESHER FEES BREAKDOWN (BY FACULTY/PROGRAM)
----------------------------------------------------
Fees listed below include Tuition, Acceptance Fee, Laboratory/Bench Fees, Field Trips, and University Miscellaneous Charges for the first academic session.

* FACULTY OF NURSING SCIENCE
  - Programme: B.N.Sc. Nursing Science
  - Fresher Total Fees: ₦1,276,000 to ₦1,559,000

* FACULTY OF MEDICAL LABORATORY SCIENCES
  - Programme: B.MLS. Medical Laboratory Science
  - Fresher Total Fees: ₦1,309,000

* FACULTY OF BASIC MEDICAL SCIENCES
  - Programmes: B.Sc. Public Health, B.Sc. Anatomy, B.Sc. Physiology
  - Public Health Fresher Total Fees: Approx. ₦401,500 - ₦839,000
  - Anatomy & Physiology Fresher Total Fees: Approx. ₦291,500 - ₦839,000
  *(Fees depend heavily on clinical posting structures and exact operational session updates).*

----------------------------------------------------
5. SAFE APPLICATION STEPS
----------------------------------------------------
1. Visit the Official UNILESA Admission Portal: https://www.unilesa.edu.ng/post-utme/info
2. Pay the standard Post-UTME registration fee of ₦2,000 via the portal's secure gateway.
3. Upload your O'Level results and track your admission status on JAMB CAPS.

⚠️ SECURITY WARNING: Do not hand over cash or pay money to anyone claiming to be a school agent or into personal bank accounts. Only pay through the school portal.
